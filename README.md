# ClearSight

**An AI-assisted markdown and exit-stock decision copilot for multi-store seasonal retailers.**

Capstone submission for MBA ZG583, Management of AI Products.
Fiza Tahreen (2024BC26584), individual submission.

ClearSight forecasts how much of a seasonal item will still sell in each store cluster at
different discount depths and timings, ranks the items most at risk, and recommends one option
with a stated confidence level. It recommends, and a person decides. It never changes a price,
and where it lacks signal it shows no recommendation at all and says why.

**Recommendation to the review panel: fund a backtest with a hard gate, not a build.** The
reasoning is in Section 10 of the Capstone Project Report.
